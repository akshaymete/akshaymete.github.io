Research Projects

The Exploration-Exploitation Trade-Off in Reinforcement Learning

Reward Biased Maximum Likelihood Estimation (RBMLE) algorithm, a online model-based RL algorithm

  • Addresses excessive exploration in UCB algorithms
  • Incorporates controlled exploration without explicit confidence intervals
  • Promising performance in MDPs, linear time series, safe RL, multi-objective RL and RLHF for Language Models

Reinforcement Learning for Auto-Regressive Time Series

PIECE, a self-tuning regulator for linear time series with \( \mathcal{O}(\log T) \) regret.

  • First \( \mathcal{O}(\log T) \) regret bound for self-tuning regulation
  • Outperforms traditional control algorithms
